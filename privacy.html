MorningVow

Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

MorningVow is designed to keep personal morning routine data on your device while using a small set of service providers for subscriptions, analytics, and diagnostics.

Data stored on your device

Your alarms, app preferences, prayer selections, wake history, streaks, and the name you enter during onboarding are stored locally. MorningVow may read calendar and health information only after you grant the relevant Apple permission. This information remains in Apple system stores and is not sent to MorningVow's analytics or subscription providers.

Data collected by service providers

  • RevenueCat: a generated app user identifier and purchase history used to provide and restore subscription access.
  • Mixpanel: the same generated app user identifier and product interaction events used to understand app usage and purchase-flow performance.
  • Sentry: crash, performance, and diagnostic information used to identify and fix technical problems. Default personal-information collection is disabled.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, subscription management, notifications, permissions, and other Apple platform services are handled under Apple's privacy terms.

MorningVow does not use this data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. It does not sell personal data.

Permissions

Notifications are used for alarms and wake checks. Calendar and health access are optional and read-only. Screen Time access is optional and is used to shield apps selected by you. You can change permissions in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

You can remove locally stored app data by deleting MorningVow from your device. Apple retains purchase records as required to operate the App Store. RevenueCat, Mixpanel, and Sentry retain service data according to their applicable retention settings and policies. To request help with service-side data associated with your generated app identifier, use the support page.
